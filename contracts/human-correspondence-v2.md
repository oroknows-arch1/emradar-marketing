# Human correspondence v2 — Semiconductor For You behaviour

The existing editorial writer, independent verifier, human_ready_email renderer,
capability gate and PUBLICATION_REVIEW are bounded workers in the same persistent
graph and feedback loop. No additional provider call or distribution path.

Every initial publication email uses six functions: verified named or neutral
 greeting; concise Sean Walker/EMRADAR identity; one short scan finding paragraph
with exact evidence state and what stood out; one evidence-backed contribution
paragraph summarizing material unresolved questions; a natural question giving
this publication agency over a destination-native contribution; a bounded sourced
draft offer and natural sign-off. The approved reference sets structure, rhythm,
context density and approximate length, never semiconductor facts or fixed recipient.
Reason/development/insight/proposition/question are exact body excerpts; reason
may share a destination-specific contribution or recipient question. No generic
format survey. Each destination gets an independently evidence-derived angle.

Target 110–150 words for the COMPLETE email, normally maximum 165 or a lower
verified destination limit. Writer targets 82–122 body words including the offer;
renderer adds about 28 words. No research-report density, statistic dump, full
causal-chain exposition, source URLs, appended Supporting source(s), citations or
 evidence lists in first contact. Warning preview uses the same concise identity
and source-free rendering. Sources belong to internal review metadata.

VERIFIED owner-authorized capability: campaign_specific_finished_sourced_draft.
English offer: "If useful, I can send a concise finished draft with sources for review."
Spanish equivalent: "Si les resulta útil, puedo enviar un borrador terminado y conciso con fuentes para su revisión."
Bounded to one finished sourced draft for the campaign and destination on request.
It is not ongoing monitoring, continuous coverage, indefinite updates, interviews
or a subscription service. Capability inventory enumerates this exact offer;
unsupported services remain prohibited. Sender remains Sean Walker, EMRADAR,
through the approved oroknows@gmail.com account.

ALL original source uncertainty is preserved verbatim in qualifications indexed
by source_index, and in evidence_binding with source facts, URLs, evidence IDs,
state and revision. It need not all appear in the email. The contribution summarizes
material uncertainty necessary to keep the short first contact truthful. Independent
verification checks factual entailment, complete internal uncertainty, truthful
summary, original destination fit and complete capability inventory. No unresolved
fact may become established fact. Evidence remains source truth.

Review hashes bind exact rendered content, destination and internal evidence.
Non-source-truth editorial warnings remain visible for owner preview, never hidden
or sent. Historical sent artifacts/receipts are immutable. Old unsent format may
be explicitly superseded; valid X copy, visual and combined artifact are reused.
No external action before exact owner approval. Preserve costs and campaign identity.
Communication features persist into receipts/outcome analysis without unsupported
causal claims. correspondenceProbe remains pure TEST_ONLY_NOT_SENT.
