# Editorial Copy System v1

## Purpose
Produce original, publication-grade copy from verified source material. This is a quality system, not a style-cloning system.

## Editorial synthesis
Use these transferable editorial qualities:
- factual discipline: lead with what is known; attribute claims; remove unsupported certainty;
- systems thinking: connect cause, bottleneck, consequence and second-order effects;
- immediacy: explain what changed and why it matters now;
- thesis compression: give the reader a clear governing idea without forcing cleverness;
- ground truth: connect abstract systems to real places, industries and people where evidence permits;
- digital clarity: short purposeful sections, strong mobile readability and useful headings;
- narrative restraint: show consequences through concrete detail before explaining them.

These qualities may be informed by widely used mainstream editorial practices, but output must never imitate, mimic, paraphrase toward, or claim the voice of any named publication or journalist.

## House rules
1. Plain language first. Precision beats flourish.
2. Open on the change, tension, consequence or strongest verified fact.
3. Each paragraph must advance the reader: new fact, mechanism, consequence, qualification or useful context.
4. Separate observation, sourced fact, inference, uncertainty and fiction.
5. Never manufacture confidence, drama, quotes, people, causation or market significance.
6. Prefer concrete nouns and active verbs. Remove generic hype and AI-sounding filler.
7. Explain mechanisms: not merely that something matters, but how it changes supply, demand, cost, behaviour, work, place or opportunity.
8. Preserve uncertainty when it is the truthful result.
9. End with the consequence, unresolved question or next thing worth watching; do not force a conclusion.
10. Destination adaptation may change length, structure and emphasis, never evidence or certainty.

## Copy modes
- report: connected explanatory reading layer;
- article: publication-grade standalone analysis;
- brief: compressed facts + mechanism + consequence;
- social: one defensible idea with enough context to stand alone;
- contribution: fill a destination's information gap without pretending endorsement;
- narrative: human-scale consequence writing with evidence/fiction boundaries intact.

## Quality gate
PASS only when copy is:
- factually bounded;
- original in wording and structure;
- clear on first read;
- specific rather than generic;
- proportionate to the evidence;
- recognisable as the product's own voice;
- free of publication imitation and generic finance/AI hype.

Product-specific voice rules extend this contract and override tone choices, but never weaken evidence boundaries.

# Marketing Engine Editorial Copy Contract v1

This repository's editorial_intelligence worker uses the shared Editorial Copy System as its base writing capability.

## Role
Convert a product's verified source material into original master copy, then adapt that copy to destinations without cloning destination voice.

## Product-agnostic rule
The engine does not own EMRADAR's, Atlasoquence's or any future product's identity. It consumes the product's copy profile and preserves it.

Pipeline:
**source product -> evidence/state -> product copy profile -> editorial master copy -> quality gate -> destination adaptation**

## Destination adaptation
May learn:
- audience knowledge level;
- expected length and format;
- useful framing;
- terminology;
- insertion point;
- headline/lede conventions at a general structural level.

Must not:
- mimic a named publication or journalist;
- rewrite evidence to fit a destination;
- hide uncertainty for engagement;
- imply endorsement or affiliation;
- flatten every product into one generic marketing voice.

When a product has no copy profile, use the shared Editorial Copy System as the neutral fallback and mark product voice as UNDEFINED rather than inventing one.
